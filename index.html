The GPM truth

The sticker flow assumes a 35 F rise to the 120 F setpoint. Cold inlet water stretches the rise, and the honest flow shrinks by exactly the same ratio.

The morning truth

A shower asks about 2 GPM. Put the honest flow next to the simultaneous showers and the margin tells you whether the morning works, barely works, or hands someone the cold surprise.

The burner truth

Every gallon per minute through every degree of rise costs 8.34 pounds of water times the rise times sixty. The GPM sticker and the BTU sticker were never in the same room.

What it never does

It never sells you the bigger unit, never promises endless hot water, and never touches the gas line or the venting - the norms are labeled, and your plumber owns the install.

Tankless Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
